noti-fi panel: the combined redesign

For each part of the panel: what each of the three options in the options document does well, what it lacks, what all three miss, and one combined design that keeps the strengths and fills the gaps. Every combined design follows four rules: choose, don't type; see the effect before saving; show where a setting comes from (theme, zone, app or rule); every action is reachable by keyboard and screen reader.

1. App shell and navigation 2. Overview 3. History 4. Apps 5. Zones 6. Rules 7. "How it looks and behaves" 8. Timeouts and timing 9. Focus modes 10. Appearance 11. Settings (General + System) 12. Shared controls 13. Build order
